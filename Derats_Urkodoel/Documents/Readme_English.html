Introduction

Urkodoël is an anarchilich found in Athkatla's Graveyard District, in the dead end near Uncle Lester.

She offers clerics, druids and shamans, including multiclass characters, an unusual bargain: learn arcane spells by permanently sacrificing some of their maximum hit points.

Urkodoël charges no gold for these lessons. Instead, the hit point cost is permanent.

Learning Spells

The standard cost is 1 maximum hit point per spell level. In return, Urkodoël also grants one additional spell memorization slot at the level at which the spell is learned.

Example: a shaman learns Fireball (level 3), permanently loses 3 maximum hit points, and gains one additional level 3 divine spell slot.

Level 8 and 9 arcane spells, as well as epic spells (HLAs), are learned and memorized as level 7 divine spells. Their cost is still based on their original spell level.

Available Spells

Urkodoël offers the base game’s arcane spells, as well as those from the following mods if they are installed before Urkodoël:

  • Sword Coast Stratagems (SCS) — nine additional arcane spells
  • Icewind Dale — through the SCS or IWDification
  • The Interplan
  • Made in Heaven Spellpack (HLAs are offered as epic spells)
  • Olvyn's Spells (HLAs are offered as epic spells)
  • Refinements (epic spell)
  • Saradas Magic (epic spell)
  • Courbespace
  • Summon Red Dragon (epic spell)

Installation Instructions

  1. First install any spell mods you want Urkodoël to recognize.
  2. Extract the Urkodoël files into the game directory, alongside chitin.key.
  3. Run the WeiDU installer included with the mod and choose your language.

To change the installation or uninstall the mod, run the installer again.
